How to read it. A reported association is supported when the same variant, or a proxy in linkage disequilibrium (r² ≥ 0.8, 1000 Genomes European or East Asian), has an association at P < 5 × 10⁻⁸ with the same trait or a broader or narrower ontology term, in a GWAS Catalog paper other than the claim's own. Earlier means that genome-wide report was published before the claim. The matched null is the share of frequency-matched random variants that would be supported for the same traits. A claim is testable when its trait has any association in the GWAS Catalog.

Limits. Claims were read from titles and abstracts by a language model and checked on a blinded sample (precision 92% for reported associations, 75% for reported absences). Support does not require the same allele or direction. Absence of genome-wide support is not evidence of no effect. Numbers here pool all papers; the estimates in the paper use held-out papers.
